Privacy Policy

OwlFly · Last updated November 16, 2026

This policy describes how OwlFly handles information in the OwlFly Android game and the public website at owlfly.app.

Gameplay data stored on your device

OwlFly saves gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device, including high scores, run statistics, achievements, unlocked sky themes, and settings such as audio and reduced motion. This information is used to remember your progress and preferences. The current game does not require an account or transmit these gameplay records to an OwlFly-operated server.

Personal information and permissions

The current Android game does not ask for a name, email address, payment details, precise location, camera, microphone, or contacts to play. Its Android manifest includes Internet permission so the application can access web resources as needed. OwlFly does not currently display advertising or offer in-app purchases.

Website access and service providers

When you visit owlfly.app, your browser sends normal network information, such as IP address and browser request metadata, to the services delivering the website. Website hosting, network, and security service providers may process and retain access or security logs to deliver and protect the site under their respective policies. OwlFly does not sell player information. No current in-game advertising SDK is used.

Sharing and security

The current game does not upload saved scores, achievements, or settings to an OwlFly backend. Website delivery providers necessarily receive technical connection information to serve the site. Website traffic is served over HTTPS. Information kept in browser or application local storage remains subject to the security of your own device.

Retention and deletion

Gameplay information stored locally remains on your device until you clear the app's storage, clear the applicable browser's site data, or uninstall the app (subject to device backup behavior). OwlFly does not currently maintain player accounts or a central database of game progress to delete. Website service logs, if retained, follow the applicable provider's operational retention practices.

Children and updates

OwlFly does not require children or adults to provide personal information to play. If a future version introduces online feedback, cloud saves, analytics, advertising, or other data collection, this policy and the associated Google Play disclosures will be reviewed before release.

Privacy questions

For inquiries, use the developer contact details provided on OwlFly's Google Play listing. You can also contact the OwlFly project through GitHub Issues; do not share passwords or sensitive information in a public issue.
